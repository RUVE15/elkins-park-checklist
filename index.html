Skip these Rarely pay off

Money spent here usually raises the next owner's home value, not your price.

  • A full kitchen remodelClean, new hardware and paint gets most of the visual win for a small slice of the cost.
  • A high-end bathroom gutFix what's broken, re-caulk, clean the grout. Don't demo.
  • Finishing the basement "for resale"Do it because you want it, not because you think a buyer will pay you back.
  • Bold, personal upgradesWhat you love, the next buyer may see as something to undo.
  • Anything past the top of your streetEvery block has a price ceiling. Spend past it and you won't see it back.

Your 6-week runway Fall, winter or spring

Weeks 6-5Walk-through with me. Order any roof, sewer or radon checks. Book contractors.
Weeks 4-3Small repairs done. Paint the rooms that need it. Start decluttering.
Week 2Curb appeal, front door, bulbs, windows. Paperwork gathered.
Week 1Deep clean, blinds open, professional photos. You're ready.
Listing this fall or winter? Fewer neighbors put their homes on the market, so yours faces less competition, and the buyers still out looking are usually serious. Aiming for spring? Start this runway about six weeks before you want to go live.
